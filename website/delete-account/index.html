Account

Delete your Mica account.

How to delete your account for Mica, the browser by lessnoise, and everything stored with it.

Mica works without an account. The account is optional: it only powers Passwords & sync and Send to device, and you sign in to it with Discord. This page applies to Mica on Android and on Windows, which share the same account.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the-api.lessnoise.online/v1/account/delete in any browser.
  2. Sign in with the Discord account you use in Mica. Signing in proves the account is yours.
  3. Check the account name and choose Delete account.

The account and its data are deleted straight away, and every device is signed out.

Delete my account

Can't sign in to that Discord account any more?

Email accounts@lessnoise.online with the subject Delete my Mica account and your Discord username. We check that the account is yours and delete it within 30 days.

You don't need the app installed for either way.

What is deleted

When we delete your account, we remove everything our server stores for it:

DataWhat it is
AccountYour Discord user ID, username, avatar, banner and accent color, copied from Discord when you sign in.
Password vaultYour saved passwords. They are end-to-end encrypted on your device before upload, so the server only holds data it cannot read.
Synced settingsThe on/off preferences you chose to sync between devices.
DevicesThe name you gave each Mica install and when it was last seen, used by Send to device.
Sent tabsThe address and title of tabs sent to another device that haven't been picked up yet.
Sign-in sessionsTokens that keep your devices signed in. Deleting them signs out every device.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing is kept on purpose. We don't keep a copy of your account or vault after deletion.
  • Our database host (Cloudflare) keeps a rolling 30-day history for disaster recovery. Deleted data drops out of it within 30 days and is never restored to your account.
  • Short-lived records expire on their own whether or not you ask: sign-in requests after 10 minutes, rate-limit counters after about a minute, and unused sign-in sessions after 30 days. Rate-limit records use a one-way hash of your IP address, not the address itself.

Data that stays on your device

Your browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs, cookies and downloads never reach our server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Remove them in Mica with Settings → Clear browsing data, or by uninstalling the app.

Signing out of Mica on a device ends that device's session but doesn't delete the account. To delete the account and the data above, send the request.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Tiếng Việt

Để xoá tài khoản Mica (trình duyệt của lessnoise) và dữ liệu đi kèm, mở liên kết này, đăng nhập bằng tài khoản Discord bạn dùng trong Mica rồi chọn Delete account. Tài khoản bị xoá ngay lập tức. Nếu không còn đăng nhập được Discord, gửi email tới accounts@lessnoise.online kèm tên Discord; chúng tôi xác minh và xoá trong vòng 30 ngày.

Dữ liệu bị xoá: tài khoản (ID, tên, ảnh đại diện Discord), kho mật khẩu đã mã hoá đầu-cuối, cài đặt đồng bộ, danh sách thiết bị, các tab đã gửi và phiên đăng nhập. Không có dữ liệu nào được giữ lại; bản sao lưu của Cloudflare tự hết hạn sau 30 ngày.

Lịch sử, dấu trang và tab chỉ nằm trên máy bạn, không bao giờ lên máy chủ. Xoá chúng bằng Cài đặt → Xoá dữ liệu duyệt web hoặc gỡ ứng dụng.
